The product

Less end-of-day sorting.
More clarity through the day.

Orders, bills and payment screenshots often arrive in separate conversations. Our planned workflow brings those details together, so business owners can see what needs attention.

01 / ORDERS

Turn messages into order drafts

Extract customer, item, quantity and delivery details from incoming messages, then flag missing information for review.

02 / PAYMENTS

Keep payment records connected

Link payment evidence to the relevant customer and order. A screenshot is recorded as evidence, with payment confirmation handled separately.

03 / FOLLOW-UPS

Know what needs attention

Prepare reminders and daily summaries from recorded orders and balances, using business rules and checks before messages are sent.

Development

Starting small.
Building around real workflows.

ZeroSoleil is developing an early prototype for small FMCG businesses. The focus is practical daily operations, with business owners staying in control.

What we’re working on

We’re prototyping message-triggered automation, structured order records, payment tracking and configurable follow-ups. Telegram is the initial testing channel; WhatsApp integration is planned.

Planned AI integration

We intend to evaluate Claude for understanding business messages, extracting document details and drafting follow-ups. Calculations and action checks will use explicit business rules.

Current stage: Prototype development. No public product launch yet.

Common questions

A few details before you explore further.

Who is this being built for?

Small FMCG wholesalers and distributors who manage orders and payment follow-ups through messaging, spreadsheets or notebooks.

Can I use the product today?

Not yet. We are developing and testing the prototype. This page introduces the product direction rather than offering a live application.

Will AI make financial decisions?

The intended design uses AI to assist with understanding and drafting. Recorded transactions, calculations and outbound actions will have business rules and review checks.
